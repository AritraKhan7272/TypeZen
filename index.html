FAQ

Questions, answered

Is TypeZen free to use?

Yes. Every feature on this site — practice, tests, games, analytics, courses and certificates — runs entirely in your browser and is free. Your data is stored locally on your own device.

How is WPM calculated?

TypeZen uses the standard formula: (correct characters ÷ 5) ÷ minutes elapsed. Accuracy is calculated as correct keystrokes divided by total keystrokes, so mistakes are not hidden by speed.

What is Personalized Practice?

TypeZen records every keystroke you make. When you finish a session it looks for letters and letter-pairs you mistype most often, then builds a custom drill containing exactly those patterns.

Can I practise with my own text?

Absolutely. Open Practice → Custom, paste any text you like, and hit start. You can also save it to your Personal Library for later.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, the interface is fully responsive. That said, TypeZen is designed around a physical keyboard — desktop gives the best experience.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start typing instantly. Creating an account (stored locally) lets you keep a named profile, streak, achievements and settings.
